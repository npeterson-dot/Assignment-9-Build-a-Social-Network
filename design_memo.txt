For this assignment, I created a social network using a graph to organize people and their friendships. A graph works well for this type of system because people can have multiple connections, and friendships usually work in both directions. Instead of organizing everyone in a specific order, the graph allows each person to connect with several others. This makes it a practical choice for representing how relationships work on social media platforms.
A regular list would not work as well because it mainly stores information in a sequence and does not naturally show how different people are connected. A tree would also have limitations because it organizes information through a hierarchy with parent and child relationships. Friendships do not follow that type of structure since people can have multiple connections without anyone being above or below someone else. Using a graph makes these relationships easier to organize and manage.
One thing I noticed while building the network was how important it is to prevent duplicate people and friendships. Without those checks, the network could become confusing and contain repeated connections. I used a dictionary to store people by name and adjacency lists to keep track of their friends. This makes finding people efficient, although checking for an existing friendship requires searching through that person's friends list. Printing the network also takes more work as the number of people and friendships increases.
This assignment helped me better understand how graphs can represent real-world relationships and why choosing the right data structure matters.
